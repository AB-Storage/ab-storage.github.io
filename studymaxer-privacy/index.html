Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how StudyMaxer ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our iOS app.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: Email address and display name when you create an account.
  • Usage data: Study session activity, streak counts, and in-app interactions to power your experience.
  • Device identifiers: We use your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) to serve relevant ads, subject to your App Tracking Transparency permission choice.
  • Analytics: Aggregate, anonymized usage statistics to improve the app.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the StudyMaxer experience
  • To display advertisements (free tier) via Google AdMob
  • To process subscription purchases via Apple's StoreKit
  • To send study reminders (with your permission)

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: Ad serving. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple StoreKit: In-app purchases and subscriptions. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Backend database for study sessions and user data, hosted on secure infrastructure.

Advertising & Tracking

StudyMaxer uses Google AdMob to display ads on the free tier. On iOS 14+, we request your permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing your IDFA. You can always change your permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. StudyMaxer Pro subscribers see no ads.

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from the app's Settings screen, which permanently removes your data.

Children's Privacy

StudyMaxer is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at support@uitools.co.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll notify you of material changes through the app or via email.
